01 · The setting

Business by day. Celebrations by evening.

The Breez Hotel hosts conferences, training and workshops in its meeting halls, and weddings, ruracio, birthdays and garden parties on its lawns and under marquees. Events come with a full set-up, Wi-Fi and food and drink packages arranged with the team.
