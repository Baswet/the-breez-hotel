Hotel · Pools · Gardens · Busia, Kenya

The Breez Hotel

A garden hotel on the Kisumu–Busia Road with pools, a restaurant and bar, meeting halls and lawns made for celebrations.

Requests go straight to reception on WhatsApp or email. Nothing is charged online.

Karibu

A welcome carved in stone at the gate.

Behind the gate are palms, a pool court, gazebos on the lawn and a fountain in the courtyard. Guests come to stay the night, to swim, to meet or to celebrate.

02 · Pools

Two pools, one for the little ones

The main pool sits in a court of palms and umbrellas, with a shallow kids’ pool beside it. Order lunch and a fresh juice to your lounger.

03 · Dining

Restaurant, bar and the poolside table

Kenyan and international plates, grills from the kitchen and drinks from the bar. Breakfast is included with stays, as listed online.

04 · Meetings & events

Halls for the workshop, lawns for the wedding

Meeting halls come with a projector and screen, Wi-Fi and a full set-up. Packages are flexible and include food and drinks. The lawns and marquees host weddings, ruracio, birthdays and garden parties.
