Nº 02 — The building

The BreezHotel

Behind the brown-and-gold gate on the Kisumu–Busia Road: a three-storey building with curved bay windows, cream columns and ochre stone, a tower over the children's pool, and a courtyard of tiled-roof cottages.

The gate and building signs read “The Breeze Hotel — Karibu”; the wall sign and social media read “The Breez”. Official spelling to be confirmed.
