Nº 02 — The hotel

Behind the gate on the Kisumu–Busia Road, the hotel opens onto a courtyard of tiled-roof cottages, gazebos and a fountain on the lawn. The main building rises over the pool terrace; the restaurant and bar look onto the water.

Guests come for a night on the road to the border, a family weekend by the pool, a training week in the halls, or a wedding under a marquee in the garden.
