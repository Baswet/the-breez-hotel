The Breez Hotel

Busia · Kenya · Karibu

A brown-and-gold gate on the Kisumu–Busia Road opens onto a three-storey building of curved bay windows and cream columns, an ochre stone tower over the children's pool, and a courtyard of tiled-roof cottages and gazebos.

Signage reads “The Breeze Hotel” on the gate and building, “The Breez Hotel” on the wall sign and social media. Spelling to be confirmed.
