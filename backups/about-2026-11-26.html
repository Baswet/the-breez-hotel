A garden hotel in Busia

The Breez Hotel stands on the Kisumu–Busia Road with rooms, pools, a restaurant and bar, meeting halls and lawns for celebrations.

01
The gate

A welcome in stone

The stone sign at the entrance greets every arrival with “Karibu”, Swahili for welcome. It is the first thing you see from the Kisumu–Busia Road.

02
The building

A tower above the palms

The main building rises behind a court of palms and the pool, and its rounded tower marks the hotel from the road.

03
The garden

Gazebos, cypress and a fountain

Between the guest wings lies a lawn with thatched gazebos, flowerbeds, a fountain and a swing. It is used for quiet afternoons and for garden parties.

04
The water

A pool court

The main pool and a kids’ pool sit together, framed by umbrellas, loungers and the VIP lounge.

05
The welcome

I love The Breez

The orange wall sign carries the name the hotel uses on Facebook and TikTok: The Breez.

The area

Busia, at the border
