# Research notes

The full research dossier (hotel facts, pricing, reviews, competitors) is kept **outside this public repository**, because it contains information the hotel has not yet approved for publication. This file records how the research was done.

## Sources reviewed

- The hotel's own channels: Facebook page and TikTok account
- Google Maps and Google Hotels listing and reviews
- Booking and travel platforms: Booking.com, Expedia group sites, Trip.com, Traveloka, Kayak, Tripadvisor
- Busia competitors listed on Google Hotels
- Design references: Aman, Rosewood, The Hoxton, Ace Hotel, Hemingways, Tribe, Sarova, Serena, The Social House, Tamarind Group, and Pinterest searches for hotel web, booking UI and African hospitality branding

## Method

Each fact was recorded with its source, date accessed, whether another source agreed, a confidence level, and whether hotel management must confirm it. Prices from booking platforms are kept separate from official hotel rates, which only management can supply.

## Outcome

- Contact channels and the main facilities are confirmed by the hotel's own posts.
- Room categories, room counts, check-in times, star rating and some facilities differ between platforms and need management confirmation.
- A confirmation checklist has been prepared for hotel management.
