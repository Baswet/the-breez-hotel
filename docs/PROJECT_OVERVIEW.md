# Project overview

## Client and context

The Breez Hotel is a hotel on the Kisumu–Busia Road in Busia, western Kenya, near the Kenya–Uganda border. It offers accommodation, a restaurant and bar, an outdoor pool, conference and meeting rooms, and events.

## Audiences

- Business travellers and government or NGO staff on assignment
- Cross-border travellers between Kenya and Uganda
- Families and couples on weekends and holidays
- Conference organisers and companies booking staff accommodation
- Local guests coming for food, drinks, the pool or a celebration

## Goals

1. A clear, attractive presentation of the hotel's real facilities.
2. Simple ways to ask for a booking or a quotation: online form, phone and WhatsApp.
3. Better visibility for searches such as "hotel in Busia", "hotel with swimming pool in Busia" and "conference venue in Busia".
4. A site the hotel's staff can keep up to date (stage two adds an admin dashboard).

## Stages

| Stage | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Research | Public sources, social media, competitors, design references | Done |
| 2. Design | Three concepts, chosen direction, design system | In progress |
| 3. Frontend | All pages, responsive, interactive forms (no live back end) | Planned |
| 4. Back end | Enquiry storage, notifications, admin dashboard, optional payments | Planned |
| 5. Launch | Hotel approval of content, rates and photos; hosting and domain | Planned |

## Principles

- **Accuracy first.** Prices, room details and facilities appear only after the hotel confirms them. Until then the site asks guests to request availability or a quote.
- **No invented content.** No made-up reviews, awards, statistics or availability.
- **Permission for images.** Hotel photos are used only with written permission; stock images are labelled as atmosphere, never as the hotel.
- **Mobile and low-bandwidth first.** Small pages, optimised images, minimal JavaScript.
