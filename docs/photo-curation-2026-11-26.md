Research notes
- References studied in the browser: Bergen Harbour Hotel (hero and experiences), Passalacqua (hero,
  "place of the heart" statement, seasons feature), Rosewood ("The Light in Our Lives" feature).
- TikTok (@thebreezhotelbusia) was reviewed for direction only; no frames were extracted or reused.
- Limitation: Facebook images are compressed (max ~1280 px wide). The hero is served from the
  1280 px source; a fresh professional shoot of the pool court at golden hour would lift it further.
