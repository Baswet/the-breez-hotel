The illustrations are generic drawings for design concepts. They are not photographs or depictions of the actual hotel. The botanical renders are decorative artwork for the homepage highlights band; they are not photographs of the hotel's garden. The two Unsplash photographs are atmospheric only: they are never captioned as the hotel, carry no room category, and are listed on the site's credits page.

## Not in this repository

- **Hotel photographs and videos** (from the hotel's social media and listings) — owned by the hotel or the photographers. They will be added only with written permission.
- **Screenshots of other websites** taken during design research — kept privately for reference; they belong to their owners.
