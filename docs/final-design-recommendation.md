# Final design recommendation

**Recommendation: build on Concept A (Editorial Escape) as the base, borrowing two parts from C** — the room comparison and the phone bottom dock — and B's ruled spec sheet for the conference page.

## Why A

- It feels most like *this* hotel: the inset frame and "Karibu" spine come from the building and its sign, and the asymmetric photography suits the courtyard, which is the hotel's most distinctive feature.
- It is calm without claiming luxury the hotel doesn't offer.
- It scales well to inner pages (rooms, pool, dining, meetings) using the numbered-section pattern.

## What to borrow

| From | Part | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C | Room comparison | Helps guests choose between similar room categories |
| C | Bottom dock: Call · WhatsApp · Book | Most guests are on phones and book by WhatsApp |
| C | Gallery filters | The gallery has 86 real photos across 8 categories |
| B | Ruled spec table | Clear for conference organisers comparing halls |

## When to choose another

- Choose **B** if the hotel wants to win more conference, government and NGO business.
- Choose **C** if the priority is families, pool day-passes and events, and a brighter, more casual brand.

## Identity, experience, warmth

Every concept now opens with the hotel's name in the hero and navigation, gives the building its own section (carved sign, gate, wall sign, stone tower, bay windows), and repeats the name large in the footer. A small set of people photos — the hotel team, guests at lunch, a courtyard wedding — adds warmth without taking over. A prototype ribbon on each page flags the Breez/Breeze spelling.

## Final selection (built and published 26 Nov 2026)

The production site combines the three concepts:

- **Concept A, Editorial Escape, is the base:** ivory and stone grounds, palm green, one warm sandstone accent from the building's cladding (used sparingly, not as "luxury gold"), Cormorant Garamond with Karla, the inset frame on the home cover, double-ruled frames and the "Karibu" welcome.
- **From Concept B, Architectural Luxury:** ruled specification panels on the room spreads, the meetings specification table, the gridded meetings hero and the column-ruled About hero.
- **From Concept C, Contemporary Hospitality:** the room comparison table, gallery filters, the quick-dates bar and the mobile bottom dock (Call, WhatsApp, Book).

Each page has its own composition:

| Page | Composition |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Framed full-bleed cover with the building and the name, then the overlapping dates bar |
| About | Numbered architectural index: gate sign, tower, garden, pool court, wall sign |

| Pools | Full-bleed hero, a sticky scroll story and a collage |
