Inspiration

Where the major decisions came from

Principles were studied and rebuilt; no layout, image or asset was copied.

  1. Inset page frame (A)Hotel du Tasso's white page on a coloured mat — hoteldutassosorrento.com · rebuilt in experiment 02
  2. Ruled navigation cells (B)Grand Hotel Vesuvio's divided top bar — Awwwards · experiment 05
  3. Visible architectural grid (B)Architecture-studio sites with hairline grids (Pinterest) · experiment 12
  4. Rotating booking stampTerreno Barrio Hotel's circular "Book now" seal — terrenohotel.com · experiment 26
  5. "Karibu" as a brand wordThe hotel's own building sign, and The Social House's "Karibu Rafiki" greeting — thesocialhouse.ke
  6. Booking bar in the heroThe Hoxton, Sarova, Ace Hotel · experiment 07
  7. Bento mosaic and floating cards (C)Dribbble and Behance hotel UI shots (Valora Heights, Luvora, Casa Veramora) · experiment 17
  8. Oversized wordmarks and numeralsTengile Malamala (Awwwards Site of the Day), Pinterest edge-numeral layouts · experiment 20
