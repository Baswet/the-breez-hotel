Nº 01 — The courtyard

Busia, Kenya

A garden of cottages, a pool among the palms.

Text sits in a narrow measure inside wide page margins; notes live in the margin, not in the copy.

Margins do the work that boxes usually do. The reading column never exceeds about 62 characters, while photographs break out to the full content width — the rhythm of a magazine spread rather than a template.

Photograph: hotel courtyard, The Breez Hotel (Facebook).
