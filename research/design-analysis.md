# Design analysis

## What the best references share

1. **Space is the luxury.** Aman, Hotel du Tasso and Rosewood use few words and wide margins; the page feels calm because little competes. Generic hotel templates fill every band edge to edge.
2. **One strong frame device.** Du Tasso's mat, Vesuvio's ruled cells and Terreno's stamp each give the site a memorable shape. The device repeats everywhere.
3. **Booking is always one step away** — in the hero (Hoxton, Sarova), in the header (Ace), or as a seal (Terreno).
4. **Place-specific language.** The Social House greets in Swahili; Serena and Tribe use a three-word line. The Breez already has both: the word **"Karibu"** on its own sign and "Class. Comfort. Ambience. Serenity." in its posts.
5. **Real photography beats stock.** The award winners use their own properties' imagery throughout.

## What to avoid (seen often on Pinterest/Dribbble)

- AI-generated dusk resorts with invented infinity pools — these misrepresent and look identical to each other.
- Glass cards, neon gradients, five competing fonts.
- Luxury positioning the hotel can't support (spa, private island, butler).

## Translating to The Breez

| Property fact | Design response |
| --- | --- |
| Ochre stone cladding, cream columns | Sand ground, ochre for booking, fine cream rules |
| Courtyard of tiled-roof cottages and gazebos | Courtyard frame motif; arch mask for images |
| Two pools with blue parasols | Pool teal as accent; a dedicated pool band in every concept |
| Hotel sign says "Karibu" | Vertical "Karibu" spine (A), stamp and ticker (exp 26) |
| Many conference guests | Clear meeting/quote paths; concept B speaks to corporate guests |
| Mobile-first audience, WhatsApp bookings | Sticky bottom booking/WhatsApp bar on phones |

## The three directions

- **A · Editorial Escape** — magazine calm, asymmetric photos, numbered sections, inset double frame.
- **B · Architectural Luxury** — visible grid, ruled cells, double borders, framed photographs, capitals.
- **C · Contemporary Hospitality** — bento mosaic, floating cards, wave transitions, interactive comparison.
