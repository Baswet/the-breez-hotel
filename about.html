02 · Our story

Karibu, carved at the gate.

The Breez Hotel stands on the Kisumu–Busia Road, close to the Kenya–Uganda border. Behind the carved Karibu sign are guest rooms, a main pool and a kids’ pool, a restaurant and bar, meeting halls, and gardens with gazebos and a fountain. Guests come to stay the night, to swim, to meet and to celebrate.
